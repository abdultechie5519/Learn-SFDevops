Salesforce Relationships (Short & Simple)

Interviewer: *Can you explain Salesforce Relationships?*

Answer:
Relationships are used to connect one object with another, just like connecting two friends on social media.

---

🔗 Lookup Relationship

Answer:
A Lookup Relationship is a loose connection between two objects. Both records can exist independently.

Funny Example: 😂
👦 Boyfriend & Girlfriend 💕
They are connected, but if one leaves, the other still exists.

Example:
Contact → Account (can exist separately in many scenarios)

👉 Lookup = Optional relationship.

---

👨‍👦 Master-Detail Relationship

Answer:
A Master-Detail Relationship is a strong parent-child relationship. The child cannot exist without the parent.

Funny Example: 😂
👶 Baby & Mother
If there's no mother (parent), the baby record (child) can't exist.

Example:
Order → Order Items

👉 Master controls Child.

---

🔄 Many-to-Many (Junction Object)

Answer:
A Many-to-Many Relationship is created using a Junction Object, which has two Master-Detail relationships.

Funny Example: 😂
👨‍🎓 Students ↔ 📚 Courses

* One student can join many courses.
* One course can have many students.

So, we create a Student Course junction object to connect them.

---

🎯 Easy Difference

| Lookup                                             | Master-Detail                            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Loose relationship 🤝                              | Strong relationship 🔗                   |
| Child can exist alone                              | Child cannot exist alone                 |
| Parent deletion doesn't automatically delete child | Parent deletion deletes child            |
| Sharing/security is independent                    | Child inherits parent's sharing/security |

---

🧠 One-Line Interview Summary

> "Lookup is a loose relationship where records can exist independently, Master-Detail is a strong parent-child relationship where the child depends on the parent, and Many-to-Many is achieved using a Junction Object with two Master-Detail relationships."

💡 Memory Trick

* 🤝 Lookup = Friends
* 👶 Master-Detail = Parent & Child
* 🎓📚 Many-to-Many = Students ↔ Courses (Junction Object)
