Interview Question: What is Salesforce?

Answer:

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform that helps businesses manage their customers, sales, service, marketing, and business processes from anywhere using a web browser. Since it is cloud-based, there is no need to install or maintain servers.

Simple Example:
A company receives a customer enquiry on its website. Salesforce helps track the customer from the initial enquiry until the product is sold and even after-sales support is provided.

---

Interview Question: What is the Salesforce Platform?

Answer:

The Salesforce Platform is a Platform as a Service (PaaS) that allows developers to build custom applications without managing infrastructure. It provides tools like custom objects, automation, security, reports, APIs, and low-code development.

Simple Example:
A hospital creates a custom "Patient" application in Salesforce to manage appointments, treatments, and billing without building everything from scratch.

---

Interview Question: What is CRM?

Answer:

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a strategy and system used to manage customer interactions throughout the customer lifecycle, from lead generation to after-sales support.

Simple Example:
When you visit a car showroom, the company records your enquiry, follows up with you, sells the car, and later reminds you about servicing. All of this information is managed in a CRM.

---

Interview Question: Explain the CRM Process

Answer:

The CRM process helps businesses convert potential customers into happy, long-term customers.

Flow:

Lead → Contact → Account → Opportunity → Quote → Order → Customer Support

---

Simple Real-Time Example

A customer visits a mobile phone website.

* Lead: Rahul fills out the "Contact Us" form.
* Contact: Sales calls Rahul and verifies his details.
* Account: Rahul's company or profile is created.
* Opportunity: Rahul is interested in buying 100 mobile phones.
* Quote: Sales sends a price quotation.
* Order: Rahul confirms the purchase.
* Support: After delivery, Rahul reports an issue, and the support team resolves it.

---

One-Line Interview Answer

"Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform that helps businesses manage customer relationships, automate business processes, and build applications. The CRM process typically follows Lead → Contact → Account → Opportunity → Quote → Order → Support to manage the entire customer lifecycle."
